# Enter The Gungeon

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Chests drop **AP Items** instead of their normal loot, and some shop items are replaced with AP Items too. Each AP Item
sends the next of your location checks to the multiworld. Its name shows what it holds and who it is for before you
pick it up.

Milestones are also checks:

- clearing rooms, opening chests and spending casings
- clearing each floor, the secret floors and the Pasts
- killing enemies (a running total across runs)
- the first kill of each boss
- each **Master Round** (beat a chamber's boss without taking damage)
- optionally: using each kind of shrine, finishing a Challenge Shrine and rescuing Breach NPCs from their cells

Progress counts across runs. Dying keeps everything you have found and received.

## What is the goal of Enter The Gungeon when randomized?

Defeat the bosses chosen in your options: the Dragun, the Lich, the Pasts, the secret floor bosses, the Advanced
Dragun and the Resourceful Rat, and Agunim. You can also add:

- **Boss Hunt**: kill a set number of different bosses.
- **Token Hunt**: find a set number of Archipelago Tokens spread across the multiworld.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

- **Random guns, passives and active items** by quality tier. Each one rolls the same item every run in a given seed.
- **Pickups**: hearts, armor, keys, blanks, ammo, casings and Glass Guon Stones.
- **Persistent upgrades**: extra max health, armor, blanks, starting casings and keys, given at the start of every run.
- **Traps**: rats, a Mimic Gun, curse, fire, poison, dropping your gun and more.
- **Progression**, each of which can be turned off:
  - Progressive Chamber Access (x4): each unlocks the elevator to the next chamber
  - The Bullet That Can Kill The Past parts: Prime Primer, Arcane Gunpowder, Planar Lead, Obsidian Shell Casing
  - Sell Creep's Favor: the Sell Creep opens the grate to the R&G Dept.
  - Old Crest, Gnawed Key and Weird Egg for the Abbey, the Resourceful Rat's Lair and the Advanced Dragun
  - Paradox Mode character unlocks, and Reverse Curse reversals
  - Archipelago Tokens, for the Token Hunt goal

With **Power Logic** on, the best guns, passives and actives count toward going deeper, so you aren't expected to enter
the Hollow or the Forge with only your starting gun.

## What does another world's item look like in Enter The Gungeon?

An **AP Item**: a ring of colored orbs. Hovering it in a shop or picking it up shows what it holds and who it goes to.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

A synergy sound plays and the item is given to you or dropped beside you. Everything you have received is given again
at the start of each run, so a run always starts with your whole multiworld collection.

## What else does the mod add?

- **The ArchipelaGun menu** (F8): connect, a tracker, your received items with descriptions, hints and a console.
- **The Elevator Operator**, an NPC by each floor's exit elevator who tells you your progress and stops you when an
  elevator is locked.
- **A recap on the death screen** (F8) of what you found and received that run.
